**FAQs**
- Free hai? Yes. Fee calls are scams; Google sued GVerifier.
- Can I choose the verification method? No, Google decides: video, phone/SMS, email, live video call or postcard. An IVR line can't receive the code.
- Postcard: usually within 14 days; the code expires in 30 days. Don't edit the name, address or category, or request a new code, while waiting.
- Ghar se kaam? Use a service-area business with the address hidden.
- Keywords in the name? No, that risks suspension.
- Description: up to 750 characters, no links, promotions or prices.
