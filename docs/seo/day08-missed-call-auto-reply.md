  - message template / examples

**Why this slug (not `missed-call-follow-up-script`)**
- "missed call follow up script" returned **no** autocomplete suggestions. "missed call follow up" only gives "follow up email" and "follow up text".
- "missed call auto reply" autocompletes to "missed call auto reply message" (plus iphone/android/samsung variants), and "missed call auto reply message" is itself a suggestion.
- "missed call reply" autocompletes to "missed call reply message".
- So `missed-call-auto-reply-message` matches the phrase people actually type. The call-back script and log stay in the H1, title and content.

**Facts used (no stats)**
- WhatsApp Business app away message: Business tools → Away message; schedule options "Always send", "Outside of business hours" (needs business hours in the profile) and "Custom schedule". Seen in several third-party guides (rasayel, saysimple, chakrahq); faq.whatsapp.com did not load. Re-check official wording before quoting further.
- The away message replies to WhatsApp chats, not to normal SIM phone calls. That is why the night rule for calls is forwarding or an AI receptionist.
- Call forwarding: generic "forward when unanswered" setting. No operator codes quoted (they differ by operator and phone).
- NOT used: "x% of callers never call back", "62% of calls go unanswered", "30–50% of sales go to the first responder" and similar figures from vendor blogs. None verified against a primary source.
- Medical: page stays on phone handling only. No treatment advice in scripts or auto-replies; the night text only says to go to the nearest hospital in an emergency.
