**Policy facts** (support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114). None of these are allowed:
- incentives: discount, free goods, payment
- **selectively soliciting positive reviews (gating)**
- requesting specific content
- pressuring customers on the premises
- conflict-of-interest reviews (family, staff)
- the review link comes from: GBP → Read reviews → Get more reviews

**CONTENT FIX NEEDED:** the templates say "agar experience accha laga ho…" / "If you liked the experience…". That is conditional, i.e. selective solicitation, so ask everyone neutrally instead. Send the message after the customer has left, not at the counter.
