Questions

Before you book

How much advance do I pay to book?

A part payment confirms hotels and cabs. The balance is due before travel. Exact amounts are written on your quote.

Are flights included in the package price?

Package prices are land-only by default. Tell us your city and we’ll add the best flight options to the same quote.

Can you arrange the visa for Dubai, Bali or Thailand?

Yes. We share the document list on WhatsApp and handle the application with your package.

What if I need to cancel or change dates?

Date changes are usually possible. Cancellation charges depend on the hotels and how close to travel you cancel — the policy is shared before you pay.

Can I change hotels or add days to a package?

Always. Every package is a starting point; we re-price it the same day with your changes.

Not sure where to go yet?

Send your dates and budget. We’ll suggest two or three options that actually fit — free.
